# Product and scope
FoodWise AI — Smarter Meals | Less Waste | Greener Tomorrow.
One-line pitch: Help a canteen prepare the right quantity, distinguish untouched surplus from plate waste, and explain each decision with evidence.
User: one kitchen manager, with staff recording actual outcomes. No account system for the prototype.
Judging alignment: AI innovation through demand forecasting and grounded explanations; sustainability through prevention; practicality through an actionable preparation sheet; UX through a short decision loop.
Differentiator: a decision can be changed live, revealing expected surplus and shortage together, then traced to historical records and staff approval.
## P0
Historical CSV import; overview; attendance-aware forecast; preparation simulator; saved approved plan; actual meal entry; untouched/plate classification; fail-closed safety review; simulated recovery log; expiry alerts; grounded report; offline demo reset; temporal backtest.
## P1
Curated stock-aware recipe suggestions, ingredient bill of materials, browser print preparation sheet. Recipe suggestions must respect menu, allergen and stock constraints; expired inventory is never suggested.
## Excluded
Camera-based mass estimation, real partner booking, emissions figures without a sourced factor and boundary, clinical/nutrition advice, generalized causal discovery, continuous neural-network retraining.
