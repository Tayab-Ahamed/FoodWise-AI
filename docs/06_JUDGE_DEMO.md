# Three-minute live judging script
0:00–0:20 “FoodWise helps kitchens prevent overproduction while protecting meal availability. These records are synthetic; recovery partners are simulated.”
0:20–0:45 Show historical Friday attendance and untouched rice surplus. Open record evidence. “This is an observed pattern, not causal proof.”
0:45–1:25 Set next meal attendance and show served-demand forecast. Change preparation slider: surplus decreases, shortage warning increases. Approve a balanced plan.
1:25–1:55 Open accounting fixture: prepared 100kg; consumed 82kg; untouched 10kg; plate waste 8kg. Served=90kg. “Plate waste cannot be solved by blindly predicting only what was eaten.”
1:55–2:20 Attempt human redistribution of plate waste: server blocks it. Untouched surplus remains pending without evidence. Show complete demo review, manager approval and simulated handoff.
2:20–2:45 Show inventory expiry alert and record actual meal. Next plan incorporates the new observation. Projected cost/waste are separate from recorded quantities.
2:45–3:00 “Every preparation change shows its evidence and service trade-off. The next step is a measured pilot with a college kitchen.”
Backup: preseed data, run locally, deterministic explanations, rehearse reset, keep screenshots if laptop fails. Do not claim the synthetic dataset proves real reductions or guaranteed uniqueness.
Questions: Is it causal? No, pattern-based recommendations requiring validation. Is food certified safe? No, policy checks plus staff review. Why AI? Attendance-aware forecasting, honest backtesting, grounded explanation; safety remains deterministic. What is novel? An integrated evidence → decision → approval → outcome loop with shortage trade-offs.
